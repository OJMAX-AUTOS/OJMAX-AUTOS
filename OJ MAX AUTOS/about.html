WHO WE ARE

Moving People With Purpose & Excellence.

OJ Max Autos is a registered premium car hire and chauffeur service company committed to providing safe, reliable, comfortable, and professional transportation solutions across Lagos and Nigeria.

We specialize in luxury vehicle rentals, executive chauffeur services, airport transfers, corporate transportation, event logistics and interstate travel.

Our goal is to deliver exceptional travel experiences that combine comfort, class, safety and professionalism, ensuring that every journey is memorable and stress-free.
